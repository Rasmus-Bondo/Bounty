## Fixed

- **Redwater — a player carried by a bot Bram is now locked out of firing, abilities and reload, the same as one carried by a real Bram.** The lockout shipped on the player charge only. Bram's charge has two entirely separate implementations, and the bot one never set the status at all — so being rammed by a bot left the victim free to shoot back and dash straight out of the grab, after which the carry's own desync check dropped the pin and completed the escape. Being rammed is identical from the victim's side; they cannot tell whether the Bram hauling them has a client. The lock is now refcounted per charger in one shared place rather than written by each charge, so two Brams holding the same victim — in any combination of players and bots — cannot release each other's lock. Every existing gate picked this up unchanged: all four hunters were already asking the shared question rather than checking who pinned them.
- **Redwater — Bram's Breach Charge can no longer force its victim through walls, floors or out of the map.** Reported first as bots vanishing into the void at one canyon corner, then as players being shoved through a wooden barricade. Four independent causes, each of which hid the next:
  - **The carry wrote victims into solid geometry.** A carried victim is held `CarryDistance` (6 studs) ahead of the charger and re-teleported every frame with their velocity zeroed. The charge only ends on a surface steeper than `cos 55°`, so a *slope* stops the charger without stopping the charge — it then bulldozed the victim into the surface for the full three seconds. A zero-velocity assembly repeatedly forced inside terrain is ejected unpredictably, and on sloped ground that ejection goes downward, through the floor. The carry point is now clamped to clear space every tick on all three paths (player charger, bot charger, and the victim's own client).
  - **The clear-space test shot the gaps in thin cover.** The map's wooden cover is 1.4-stud beams and 0.6-stud panels with gaps between them, not solid wall. Measured over 216 headings at one structure, a single centre ray reported "clear" on 6% of directions where a body-sized cast collides — every one a frame that wrote the victim into a beam. All clamps now spherecast at a body half-width.
  - **A server write on a player's character was being silently discarded.** A player owns their own character's physics, so the correctly-clamped server position lost to their client every frame — measured mid-carry, the victim sat 2.9 studs from the *unclamped* point and 6.8 from the clamped one. This is why the first fix worked immediately for bot victims (server-owned) and never once helped a player. The server now takes network ownership of a player victim for the duration of the carry and drives them directly; their own client's follow loop is demoted to local prediction.
  - **The clamp cancelled itself on the victim.** The bot path cast from the charger toward the carry point — where the victim is standing — hit their body, and a character guard returned the *unclamped* position. It was a no-op from the moment it was written, which is why the two fixes above both appeared to do nothing until it was found.
- **Redwater — several enemies caught by one Breach Charge are carried side by side instead of inside one another.** Every victim on a charge was written to the same carry point and later the same settle point, i.e. interpenetrating character assemblies. Harmless in open ground, violent in a corner with nowhere to eject. Victims now take separate lanes.
- **Redwater — a slammed victim is left against the wall rather than bounced off it.** The bot path settled victims 3 studs clear, which read as a rebound; it is now 2, measured against real rigs whose geometry reaches 1.3–1.7 studs forward of the root. The correction is also applied only when a victim is genuinely past the wall face, so a slam granted at long range can never drag someone *onto* a wall they were clear of.

## Changed

- **Redwater — Bram's Breach Charge now slams on wooden scaffolding instead of plowing through it.** The forward wall test had the same single-ray blind spot as the clamp above. It now fires a centre ray *and* a body-width spherecast and accepts either, in all three implementations that must agree (server tick, the server's re-verification of a client-reported hit, and the client that reports it — a client detecting on a test the server does not share turns every reported slam into a rejected "no wall" and a 15-damage whiff). Swapping the ray for a sphere outright was tried and rejected: at one corner the sphere clips the sloped floor first and rejects it as a ramp, never reaching the rock face behind, so it detected *fewer* walls there. Running both measured zero regressions at every site tested — no angle that previously slammed has stopped. Accepted trade: a lone pole directly in the charge path now stops the charge, where a ray slipped past.

- **Bounty — the Day 1 and Day 2 daily rewards can be claimed on a phone again.** The mascot's viewport is authored above the reward tiles, so its *transparent* bounding rectangle covered 88% of those two CLAIM buttons. Marking the panel's children `Active` — which is what stops a stray press falling through and closing the panel — turned that rectangle into a tap sink, leaving an ~11px strip of each button that actually responded while the whole button still looked clickable. Days 3-7 sit clear of it, which is why it presented as "Day 2 impossible to claim, Day 1 difficult". The mascot no longer takes input, and a new full-panel sink beneath it keeps an outside press closing the panel exactly as before.
- **Bounty — every daily reward CLAIM button now meets the 44x44 minimum touch target.** All seven were sized in scale only and resolved to 15-23px tall at the smallest supported viewport (800x360), under half the minimum. Each tile gained an invisible hit area with a 44px floor rather than a bigger button: the painted buttons are untouched on every device, which matters because the Day 3 and Day 4 tiles are too short to hold a 44px button without crushing their reward text to 4px.
- **Bounty — Collection: owned hunter skins and gun skins no longer read PURCHASE after returning from a match.** (Launch issue #11.) Ownership is delivered by `SkinDataEvent`, and a push whose listener has not connected yet is dropped permanently. Three separate client scripts pull the backup `RequestSkinData` on startup — `CollectionClient`, `HuntersBuyController` and the Collection menu's `State` — but the server's 0.5s debounce is keyed per *player*, so it could not tell them apart. An earlier script's request was answered while the Collection menu was still deaf, then the menu's own request landed inside the debounce window and was thrown away, leaving ownership empty for the entire session. The debounce now suppresses only the redundant second waiter and never the payload: if the profile is loaded, the reply is always sent. Both EQUIP-button labels test `isHunterOwned(...) and isSkinOwned(...)`, which is why a hunter skin and a gun skin always failed together.
- **Bounty — the Collection menu's ownership listener no longer stays deaf for most of server boot.** `BoostService.Start()` and `CollectionService.Start()` now run 2nd and 3rd in `Main`, immediately after `DataService`. The menu's client-side `Refs` blocks on remotes those two create before it can connect its skin-ownership handler; started at the bottom of the list they left a multi-second window — the whole span after `DataService` had already loaded profiles and pushed ownership — during which the join push was routinely lost. This is the condition that made the bug above show up specifically on a teleport arrival into a freshly-booted lobby. **Any service added later that creates a remote a client `WaitForChild`s on belongs in that early group.**
- **Bounty — owned titles and emotes no longer render as locked on first arrival.** `RequestTitleState` and `RequestEmoteState` read the profile once and silently answered nothing when it had not loaded yet — which is the *normal* case arriving from Redwater, where the profile is still session-locked by the server the player just left. Both now wait for the profile (20s) the way `RequestSkinData` does, capped at one waiter per player per remote.
- **Bounty — emote ownership is now pushed on join.** It never was: join publishes attributes only, so `RequestEmoteState` was the sole delivery path and a pull that lost the race left every owned emote locked until the tab was closed and reopened.

- **Redwater — being rammed by Bram's Breach Charge now locks the victim out of abilities, firing and reloading.** (Launch issue #1.) Fortuna's Surge, Dust's Quick Draw and Vesper's Grapple Hook could all break out of a carry. A gate did exist — every hunter client's `isCombatAllowed()` and `BotCombat` already covered fire, abilities and reload — but all of them tested only `StatusStunned`, which `HunterStatusEffects:ApplyStun` writes solely on the wall **slam**. For the whole pin/carry phase no status attribute was set: bots got a movement override, while a pinned *player* got only `KnockbackGraceUntil` and an FX message their own client acted on. The three abilities named in the report are simply the roster's three **displacement** abilities — the victim moved past `ReleaseDistance` and the charge's own desync check let go. Fortuna's Recall and Vesper's Pinning Round were equally ungated and never came up because they do not move you, which is why patching the three named abilities would have been the wrong fix.
- **New shared module `HunterActionLock`** is now the single answer to "may this character act": `StatusStunned` **or** an unexpired `StatusPinnedUntil` deadline that `BreachChargeService` writes for players and bots alike. Deliberately dependency-free so a LocalScript, a server module and a bot brain can all require it. The pin is a **deadline rather than a flag** because it is written from a shared per-charge Heartbeat loop, where one throw freezes every later entry — a stranded flag would leave a player unable to fire for the rest of the round, while a deadline expires on its own whichever cleanup path was skipped. `Workspace:GetServerTimeNow()` is used because it is the synchronised clock; `os.clock()`/`tick()` would not survive the server→client hop.
- **Stun and pin are now enforced server-side, not only on the client.** Previously every ability remote validated hunter identity and rate-limited, but nothing checked stun — an honest client obeyed and nothing else had to. The check is folded into each hunter module's existing `isRoundCombatAllowed()`, which now takes an optional `player`, so all 14 call sites inherit it and future handlers following the same convention get it for free; `CardsServer` and `HighNoonModule` have no such helper and carry explicit guards. **Not for player-facing notes** — this half is an anti-cheat backstop and belongs under "backend improvements" in anything a player reads.
- One deliberate exception: Breach Charge's `WallHit` and `End` remote actions stay ungated, because they report on a charge already in flight and must still land while Bram is stunned or pinned. Only `Start` is gated. At the slam, `_resolveSlam` applies the stun *before* `_unpin`, so a victim never sees an unlocked frame between the two states.
- **Balance consequence, called out deliberately:** the lock covers all combat during the ram, not abilities alone, so a slam landed during a Dust High Noon wind-up now denies the shot and the ultimate expires unused. Consistent with how the stun already behaved, but it is a real change — the `highNoonShootRequestRemote` guard is the single line to lift if it plays badly.

**Release risk for the Breach Charge lock:** **no mirrored module touched.** `HunterActionLock` is new but lives under `ReplicatedStorage.Hunters.Shared`, which exists only in Redwater — Bounty has no `Hunters` folder at all (verified), so it is **not** a new mirror and must not be added to the 16. **Not data-affecting** — no Wallet reasons, XP curve, RP awards, pass grants or profile-shape changes; it is combat gating only and reverts cleanly with the place. Touches 14 scripts across both hunter clients and servers plus `BotCombat`; verified in Studio by the owner.

**Release risk for the Bounty fixes:** no shared modules touched — `DataService`, `Main` and `CollectionService` are all Bounty-only. `DataService` does exist in both places, but the edit is confined to the `RequestSkinData` handler; the profile template and `DATA_VERSION` are untouched, so the two copies stay aligned. **Not data-affecting** — no Wallet reasons, XP curve, RP awards, pass grants or profile-shape changes. This is delivery and timing of already-persisted state, so it reverts cleanly with the place.
