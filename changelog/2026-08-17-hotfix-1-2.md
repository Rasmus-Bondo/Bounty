## Added

- **The onboarding tutorial pays its reward a step at a time instead of as one lump at the end.**
  250 Cash and 50 Tokens are run TOTALS split across the step list — 25 Cash and 5 Tokens per
  advance on the stock 10-step lesson — with the remainder settled at the end, so a finished run
  still lands on exactly 250 / 50 whatever the step list does. Tokens are new to the tutorial
  award; the Cash total is unchanged. A step that rides its timeout still pays, like the flat
  award it replaced.

  **Reverting** restores the lump payout but not the old profile state: the first instalment sets
  `Tutorial.BonusPaid`, so an account that abandoned a run mid-lesson now reads as paid and will
  not be paid again.

  Verified by sandboxing the shipped payout functions against stubbed services across seven cases
  — full run, skipped step, a pot that does not divide evenly, quit at step 3, replay on a paid
  account, test-account replay, and a double `payOut`. **Not yet playtested end to end.**

- **Each completed step shows a reward row on the combat feed, and reward rows now carry the
  game's real Cash and Token icons.** The feed stays silent for the rest of the lesson; the
  instalment row is a named exception rather than a loosening of that rule. The icons are the
  lobby shop's own currency art and replace the `$` prefix and the word "Tokens" on **every**
  reward row, not just the tutorial's.

  **Reverting** restores the single-label row; nothing outside `CombatFeedClient` depends on the
  segment structure.

  Verified by rendering every row kind — including a text-only bounty announcement and an overlong
  one that still truncates — confirming the instalment row survives tutorial suppression while an
  ELIMINATION and a spree announcement do not, and that ordinary rows are otherwise unchanged.

- **Players can replay the onboarding tutorial from the lobby.** The HOME sign's CHANGE MODE button
  now opens a picker with DEATHMATCH and TUTORIAL; the sign shows the selection and the existing
  PLAY button starts whatever is shown. Choosing TUTORIAL sends the player solo to a freshly
  reserved Redwater tutorial server. A player in a party is refused with "Leave your party to
  replay the tutorial." — a tutorial server carries no group, so there is no shape of that teleport
  that takes a party along.

  **A replay writes nothing.** It does not spend `Tutorial.Attempts` (the first-timer dispatch
  budget, which also drives DataService's grandfather screen), does not touch
  `Tutorial.Completed`, and is paid 0 by the existing `Tutorial.BonusPaid` rule — the new
  `tutorialReplay` flag is telemetry only, and keeps veteran re-runs out of the first-time
  drop-off funnel. New `TutorialReplayService` in Bounty, deliberately separate from
  `TutorialDispatchService`: dispatch decides who is *offered* onboarding, and every gate in it
  exists to stop the lesson happening to someone twice.

  **Reverting** needs both places — Bounty loses the picker, and Redwater/Frostbite's funnel skip
  goes with it. Nothing persists, so a revert strands no account.

  Verified: all five scripts compile; the replay path's four no-write guarantees checked against
  live source; mirrors byte-compared. **Player-tested by the user; the party refusal and the
  reserve-failure ladder are not yet exercised live.**

## Changed

- **The onboarding tutorial is an offer now, not a teleport.** A first-time player used to be sent
  into the lesson the moment their profile loaded, with a full-screen card that only said "hold on".
  They now get a card that names the reward — 250 Cash and 50 Tokens, sent by the server so the
  lobby cannot advertise a figure Redwater no longer pays — and two buttons. Nothing else closes it:
  no timer, no auto-advance, no click-outside. The `ProgressSliver` that drew the old 4s
  auto-advance is deleted.

  "skip for now" declines. The first decline stamps `Tutorial.Skipped` and leaves the account
  eligible, so the offer returns on the next join; the second stamps `Completed` and it never comes
  back. `Tutorial.Attempts` is deliberately untouched — a decliner was never dispatched, and
  spending an attempt would eat the teleport retry budget and make DataService's grandfather screen
  read them as already in the flow. No new profile key.

  **Reverting** un-skippable onboarding is safe code-wise but not data-wise: accounts that declined
  carry `Skipped` / `Completed`, and a revert will not re-offer them. It also drops the
  `"__request"` pull that makes a client-triggered teleport safe — that pull is the whole reason the
  choice could come back, since the race it answers (a `FireClient` at a listener that has not
  connected yet is silently dropped) is what got the choice removed in August.

  Verified: all four scripts compile; the offer card screenshot-checked on desktop 1658x678, Galaxy
  A06 800x360 and iPad 9th gen 1080x810, with the scrim A/B captured to confirm it still dims and
  still blocks the menu. **The flow itself is not yet playtested end to end.**

- **The TRAINING COMPLETE card shows both currencies.** `REWARD [cash] 250 [token] 50`, using the
  lobby HUD's own two currency assets. Each chip is driven by its own amount, so a run that paid one
  shows one and a zero-zero payout still hides the row rather than claiming a reward that was not
  paid.

  **Reverting** shows Cash only, under-reporting a run that paid both.

  Verified by render; the row's five width fractions were re-cut and measured height-bound at 26px.

- **`STUDIO_TEST_HUNTER` is back to `nil` in both match places.** A Studio-only spawn override was
  left holding `"Vesper"` in Redwater and `"Dust"` in Frostbite — the only drift in the match tier,
  and enough on its own to stop `/preflight` at gate one. `IsStudio()` meant it could never reach a
  player, which is exactly why it sat there; the mirror check does not care.

  **Reverting** is meaningless — `nil` is the shipped state and normal profile-based hunter
  resolution is what returns.

- **Tutorial test switches: two ON, one cleared.** `TEST_ACCOUNT_BONUS_REPLAY` (mirrored into
  Frostbite) and Bounty's `TEST_ACCOUNTS_REPLAY` remain on, so three edits to undo.
  `TUTORIAL_FORCE_MODE` went back to `false` on 2026-08-17 — see the metadata bullet: DEV counts as
  non-live, so it is a DEV blocker rather than untidiness. Tracked as open item 119.

  `TEST_ACCOUNT_BONUS_REPLAY` is why the new feature looked broken on its first test. The two replay
  switches are independent, and with Bounty's on and this one off a listed account replays the lesson
  **unpaid** — an unpaid step pays nothing and therefore fires no reward row. That used to cost one
  reward line at the very end; against a per-step award it costs every step's feedback. Whoever turns
  these off should decide whether the two ought to be welded together. (They are both on now, which
  is the combination a DEV soak wants.)

- **The HOME camera sits three studs further back**, so the whole PLAY sign clears the left edge on
  a 4:3 tablet instead of overhanging it by ~60px. Solved by sweeping the distance: 2 studs is the
  first that clears, 3 leaves margin clear of the sidebar.

  **Reverting** re-crops the sign on 4:3. The camera part is read by three scripts — the loading
  screen's final shot, the HOME tab, and the lobby dummy's facing — and all three apply the same
  180° yaw, so they moved together. Rotation is unchanged.

  Verified on iPad 9th gen and iPhone 16 landscape, and confirmed by the user on tablet.

## Fixed

- **The tutorial's Token award never reached the completion card on an ordinary run.** The amount
  rode `stampReturnTeleportData`, which only the EXIT TRAINING path reads, and was missing from
  `sendHome`'s own `SetTeleportData` and from `RoundController`'s solo drain-retry — so every player
  who finished the lesson normally was paid 50 Tokens and told about 250 Cash. Both paths now carry
  `tutorialTokens`. Same bug shape as the 2026-08-11 one where a retried return arrived with no
  completion flag at all: three exits from a tutorial server, and each has to promise the same thing.

  **Reverting** silently drops the Token line again — the payout is unaffected, which is what makes
  it easy to miss.

  Verified by read-back of all three exits; `TutorialDirector` and `RoundController` re-checksummed
  byte-identical Redwater ↔ Frostbite after the edits (77094 / 100589 bytes).

- **The PLAY and CHANGE MODE signs were nearly unhittable on a 4:3 tablet.** Their screen hit
  targets collapsed to 5x98 and 4x67 pixels against true sizes of 440x143 and 429x128, because the
  projection discarded any corner of the sign that fell outside the viewport — and on 4:3 the sign
  overhangs the left edge, so half its corners were dropped. Off-viewport corners are now kept;
  only points behind the camera are rejected.

  The mobile hit boxes are also placed from the projected bounds every frame instead of authored at
  a fixed 35% x 18% of the screen, which had been eyeballed against one device and covered far more
  than the sign it stood in for. CHANGE MODE gained its own box; the two overlapped by ~20px, which
  resolved by draw order and put the picker under the bottom edge of PLAY, so the contested strip is
  split.

  **Reverting** restores the oversized PLAY box and the collapsed targets.

  Verified by measuring both rects at 4:3 and 16:9 before and after (322x108 and 315x96 on tablet,
  zero overlap), screenshot-confirmed on the signs, and player-tested by the user.

- **Clicking outside the CHANGE MODE picker did nothing.** The dismiss was firing correctly the
  whole time — the same click also reached the 3D sign behind the scrim and reopened the panel
  within the frame. The signs are hit-tested by a projected screen rect that knows nothing about GUI
  layering, and the flag that normally covers that is not dependable on a touch release. The sign
  click path now refuses while the picker is open.

  The picker's own dismiss was rewritten to match `ReworkedSettingsGui`'s backdrop, which is the
  click-outside dismiss in this lobby that has always worked: dismiss on the press, and measure the
  press against the card's rect rather than trusting hit-test routing.

  **Reverting** brings back a picker that cannot be dismissed by tapping outside.

  Verified: both scripts compile, debug instrumentation removed, and player-tested by the user.
