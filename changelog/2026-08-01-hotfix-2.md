## Fixed

- **The lobby's 3D party-invite buttons could be dead for a player's entire session.**
  The top row of the dashboard at **546 warnings** — *"Device3DClickHandler could not find
  Workspace > InviteParts"* — and a different bug from the invite-button fix in the hotfix
  before it, which is why it survived it. `Mobile3DClickHandler` captured the container **once at
  script load** with a bare `FindFirstChild` over three candidate names. `ModelStreamingMode
  = Persistent` guarantees `InviteButtons` *arrives*; it does not guarantee it exists on the
  line a StarterPlayerScript runs first. Lose that race and the local stays `nil` for the rest
  of the session: the buttons never work again, and every subsequent tap re-warns behind only
  the 0.18 s click cooldown, which is how three hours produced 546 lines.

  The first-seen timestamp (23:09 on launch night, with the hotfix already live) points at the
  Folder→Model conversion having *shifted* replication timing and made a pre-existing race
  start losing. The container is now resolved lazily through `getInvitePartsFolder()` on every
  use, cached, and re-resolved if it is ever missing or reparented; a `task.spawn` per candidate
  name warms that cache with a bounded 30 s `WaitForChild` (parallel, so the timeouts cannot
  stack). The warning is now once per session and names the script correctly.

  *Player-facing:* "fixed the party invite buttons sometimes not working in the lobby."

- **`Workspace.LobbySpawns` got the streaming treatment `InviteButtons` already had.**
  Same root cause as that fix and missed by it. `LobbySpawns` was still a plain Folder, so its
  four slot markers streamed by distance while the character spawns at the origin, producing
  infinite-yield warnings on `LobbySpawns:WaitForChild("Player1")`. `LobbySpawn` resolves the
  folder, `CameraFolder.CameraPart` and all four slots through a no-timeout `WaitForChild` chain
  at the very top, so a slow stream-in stalled the remaining ~900 lines — lobby camera and every
  dummy included — without logging anything. Now a Model with `ModelStreamingMode = Persistent`
  (verified: no part moved), and the chain runs through a `requireChild` helper with a bounded
  30 s wait that warns and bails instead of hanging silently.

- **Vesper's scoped-look damping warned on a transient and then stayed quiet forever.**
  22 warnings of *"Camera rotateInput unavailable"*. `VesperScopeController`'s render-step bind
  warned the **first** frame `rotateInput` was not a `Vector2` — but the PlayerModule swaps
  `activeCameraController` on state changes, so it is legitimately absent for a frame or two
  around scope-in. A blip that recovered on the next frame therefore burned the once-only
  warning and left a permanent claim that mobile look was undamped. It now requires
  `ROTATE_INPUT_GRACE_FRAMES` (30, ~0.5 s) of *consecutive* misses, with the counter reset on
  every successful frame and on each scope-in. No change to the damping itself.

  Diagnostic-only: these 22 were almost certainly transients rather than broken sessions.

- **Anti-cheat kicks are now diagnosable after the fact.** Two players were kicked in the
  launch window and neither could be judged from the logs, because `MovementChecker` recorded
  per-flag detail with `print` — which the error dashboard does not capture. All that reached it
  was the ≥3 crossing and a bare *"kicked for movement violations"*, with no hunter, no humanoid
  state, no grounded flag and no indication of whether an ability grace was live.

  Every flag now carries a `describeContext` string: `SelectedHunter`, humanoid state,
  `FloorMaterial`, resolved `WalkSpeed`, the `grounded` probe result, measured horizontal speed,
  and each grace attribute rendered **relative to the moment of the flag**. A negative grace
  (`KnockbackGraceUntil-0.42s`) means the window had already closed when the flag fired, which
  is exactly the signature of a grace sized to an ability's duration instead of held until
  landing. The kick warning now dumps the full flag history, each entry stamped with seconds
  since spawn, weight, running total, reason and context.

  The kick emits **two** warnings. The first is the original stable line, left in its exact
  previous form so the dashboard keeps aggregating it into one countable row per player — folding
  the history into it would make every kick unique and destroy the tally. The second carries the
  total, the triggering reason and the history. `FLAG_HISTORY_LIMIT` is 6, which is the whole run
  (threshold 5, lightest flag weight 1, so at most five flags precede a kick); a worst-case detail
  line measures ~1,120 characters, against ~2.5 KB at the first-cut limit of 12, which the
  dashboard would likely have truncated.

  Ground state moved above the speed checks so all of them can carry it — the raycast count is
  unchanged. Detection behaviour, weights, ceilings and the threshold are all untouched; this is
  logging only. Note that `print`-level per-flag detail is still invisible to the dashboard by
  design: promoting every weight-1 blip to `warn` would flood it, so the buffer is what carries
  that detail to the kick.

- **Bram's charge no longer gets him — or anyone he throws — kicked by the movement anti-cheat.**
  The same defect the Vesper grapple fix addressed, in the one place that fix did not reach.
  `BreachChargeService` sized `KnockbackGraceUntil` to the *ability*: `Time + 0.8` at charge
  start, a flat `0.8` refreshed per tick, and `PinGraceBuffer` (0.8) for victims. A grace sized
  to a duration is wrong whenever the ability leaves the character airborne, because the velocity
  `MovementChecker` samples persists until they hit the ground — a function of how far they fall,
  not how long the ability ran.

  The calibration is what makes it bite: **`ChargePeakSpeed` is 42 against a 40 studs/s
  horizontal ceiling**, so the charge is only legal *while the grace holds*. The moment it lapsed
  mid-flight, Bram was flagged at ~42 on every 0.5 s sample — and because violations only decay
  on a sample that is both unflagged **and grounded**, an airborne player cannot recover. Five
  weight-1 flags is a kick, so 2.5 seconds. This matches the launch-window kick logged at
  **`horizontal speed 42.5 > ceiling 40.0`** almost exactly, weight-1 flags only and no
  teleport-scale position deltas — which is what a 42-studs/s ability looks like, and not what a
  speed exploit looks like.

  New shared `ReplicatedStorage.Hunters.Shared.MovementGrace` generalises Vesper's
  `_beginGrappleGrace`: a floor, a poll that extends while `FloorMaterial == Air`, a
  `LandingSettle` hold after touchdown, and a `MaxGraceTime` abuse ceiling. Tokens key on
  **(character, attribute)** rather than character, so a knockback landing mid-grapple cannot
  retire the grapple's watcher. Wired at the caster's charge start and in `_unpin` — the single
  funnel for every release path — plus `BotCombat:_releasePin` for players pinned by a Bram bot.
  Dials live in `GrudgekeeperConfig.BreachCharge.Pin` (`GraceMaxTime` 6, `GraceLandingSettle`
  0.4, `GracePollInterval` 0.2); the ceiling must exceed the 3.8 s floor or the floor is clamped.

  `GrappleHookService` deliberately keeps its own implementation this round — it is a working
  live fix and refactoring it in the same pass as a new one is not worth the risk. Migrating it
  onto `MovementGrace` is the obvious follow-up.

  *Player-facing:* "fixed a bug that could disconnect you while using Bram's Breach Charge."

## Changed

- **`MovementChecker.MAX_HORIZONTAL_SPEED` raised 40 → 50.** The ceiling was set above the
  fastest *walk* (Fortuna's 24 × 1.25 sprint = 30) but **below** the fastest *ability*: Bram's
  `ChargePeakSpeed` is 42. An ability that outruns the ceiling makes detection depend entirely on
  grace coverage being perfect — any gap is an instant false kick rather than a near miss, which
  is precisely what happened. 50 puts real headroom above the fastest legitimate movement while
  staying far below anything a speed exploit is worth running.

  This does not replace the grace fix above; the two are complementary. Release and slam
  velocities still exceed the charge peak, and the vertical (65) and position-delta (70) checks
  are untouched.

- **Two dead GUI trees deleted from Bounty's `StarterGui`, cutting it by 17%.** The seven
  remaining infinite-yield warnings (`BattlepassGui`, `QuestCompleteNotification`,
  `ReplicatedStorage.Events`, `CameraFolder`, and the invite/spawn parts fixed above) are all

  that much less before `PlayerGui` is populated, which shrinks the window those warnings fire
  in. `ReworkReworkedBuyMenu` is now 5,054 descendants — **58% of what remains** — and is the
  obvious next target. `ChestTest`, `CinematicTest`, `MobilePlayButtonBackup` and
  `RankLeaderboardGuiLegacy` are also flagged as probably dead but were **not** audited.
