- **`ServerScriptService.AntiCheat.CombatChecker` — combat anti-cheat, detection only** (both match
  places). Second script in `AntiCheat`; `MovementChecker` was previously the only one and combat
  had no checker at all. **Not for player-facing notes — "backend improvements" and nothing more.**

  It attaches its **own** `OnServerEvent` listener to `Dust.FireBullet`, `Vesper.FireBullet` and
  `Bram.FireShotgun` alongside each hunter module's. Roblox runs connected listeners independently
  and every handler body is `pcall`'d, so it **cannot block, alter or delay a shot** — that
  isolation is why it was safe to ship in the same pass as a publish. Fortuna is deliberately
  unwatched: her basic is a projectile with a different payload shape.

  Two signals, only one of which scores. **Aim divergence** (weight 2): flags when ≥60% of a 12-shot
  window has the claimed direction more than 70° off the character's actual facing. A ratio over a
  window, not a per-shot test — one wide shot is a flick plus lag. Shots taken while `IsDashing` are
  not sampled at all, because `RevolverDashService` clears `AutoRotate` for the roll and a
  legitimate mid-dash shot genuinely points away from the body. Threshold 5, decaying 1 per 20 s of
  clean play; `warn` so it reaches the error dashboard, with the full flag history at threshold.

  **Cadence regularity is observation only and carries no weight** (`CADENCE_SCORES = false`): it
  warns when the mean absolute deviation of 8 intervals falls under 12 ms, and never scores.
  **`AutoFireLocked` is not a usable gate and the first draft of this script wrongly treated it as
  one.** All four hunter clients publish it via `player:SetAttribute` from a **LocalScript**, and
  client-side attribute writes do not replicate upward — the server never sees it, and nothing
  server-side reads it. Replicating it deliberately would not fix it either: it would become a
  client-controlled flag a cheat could assert to silence the signal. Since mobile auto-fire is a
  genuine fixed-interval metronome, the signal cannot score until there is a server-inferred way to
  exclude it. It stays in to gather calibration data.

  **`ENFORCE_KICKS = false`, and it should stay false until live data says otherwise.** One
  false-positive path is known and unsolved: a client whose replication stalls leaves the server
  holding a frozen copy of the character while shots arrive carrying fresh directions, which reads

- **Team spawn houses with one-way force-field doors, on both maps.** Each side spawns inside its
  own building — `RedTeamBase` / `BlueTeamBase` — from twelve authored markers per house on both

  **The houses are solid to gunfire.** They are built almost entirely from `Wood`, which the shared
  penetration rules treat as shoot-through cover by material, so a spawn house would otherwise have
  been a building you could kill people through. Each base model carries a `Penetrable = false`
  attribute, which is the documented highest-priority override and applies to every descendant at
  once — 314 parts per house in Redwater, 236 in Frostbite. Ordinary wooden cover elsewhere on the
  map is untouched.

  **A base's doors are solid to the enemy and open to its owners, for bodies and bullets alike.**
  You walk through your own door and shoot through it; the enemy's stops both. That is one
  PhysicsService collision matrix rather than two mechanisms — rigs wear their side's group, door
  slabs wear theirs, and a shot's raycast wears a third — so the thing that decides who may walk
  through is the same thing that decides whose bullets cross. Every hitscan weapon inherits it from
  the single shared penetration call, server and client, so tracers and hits agree. Doors carry a
  pathfinding override, because the navmesh has no concept of collision groups and would otherwise
  wall bots out of every base.

  system has more casts than that: the revolver's server-side obstruction check, Bram's near-miss

  ray builder, and Bram's client tracer fan are all either bare casts or penetration calls with no
  shooter. Every one of them defaulted to the `Default` group, which passes through every door — so
  the server would validate a shot straight through an enemy force field while the client's own
  tracer stopped at it. The group is now set where each params object is BUILT, which is the only
  place that covers ordinary raycasts, spherecasts and the penetration walk at once.

  **Spawn clearance is a solid-volume test, not a raycast.** A Roblox raycast whose origin sits
