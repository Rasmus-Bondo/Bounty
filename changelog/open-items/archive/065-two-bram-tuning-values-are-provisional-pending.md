# 65 — Two Bram tuning values are provisional, pending play

[Open items index](../../OPEN-ITEMS.md) · [Conventions](../../CONVENTIONS.md)

**Closed 2026-08-11** — owner review of the standing-concern list.

---

**Two Bram tuning values are provisional, pending play.** The clash stun is deliberately shorter
than a wall slam's — a slam is a fight someone lost and costs health too, a clash is symmetrical
and both players have already forfeited the ability and its cooldown. Weigh against the fact that
two players stunned together in a free-for-all are an easy double kill for anyone watching. The
charge's 1-second cancel lockout is likewise a tuning value rather than a constant in the logic.
**Deliberately not done:** knocking clashing Brams apart with an impact effect — a new mechanic
needing knockback, stun and FX tuning for a bug whose whole cost is two charges failing to
resolve. *(raised: #23 / #6, [Hotfix 6](../../2026-08-05-hotfix-6.md))*
