# 64 — The kill feed may still look inset on a real phone, and that is the safe area, not the alignment

**Closed 2026-08-11** — owner review of the standing-concern list.

---

**The kill feed may still look inset on a real phone, and that is the safe area, not the
alignment.** The feed keeps clear of device notches and rounded corners, a strip Studio cannot
model or measure. Removing it risks the feed sliding under a notch. Also still deferred and now
much less pressing: making each line shrink to fit its contents — short lines draw a wider
translucent bar than they need, but with the text right-aligned the empty part sits on the left
where nothing lines up anyway. *(raised: #14, [Hotfix 6](../../2026-08-05-hotfix-6.md))*
