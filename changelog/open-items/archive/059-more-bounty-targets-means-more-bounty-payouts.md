# 59 — More bounty targets means more bounty payouts — a balance question, not a bug

[Open items index](../../OPEN-ITEMS.md) · [Conventions](../../CONVENTIONS.md)

**Closed 2026-08-11** — owner review of the standing-concern list.

---

**More bounty targets means more bounty payouts — a balance question, not a bug.** Bots backfill
against the player count, so the quietest servers now carry the most bounty heads, and the "Kill
the Bounty Target" daily is much easier to finish. Nothing about the reward changed. Worth a look
once it has been played. Also: **bounty *kills* per match now include bots**, so that internal
figure no longer moves with the human headcount and **cannot be compared against matches before
this release**. Bounties *held* is still players only.
*(raised: #21, [Hotfix 6](../../2026-08-05-hotfix-6.md))*
