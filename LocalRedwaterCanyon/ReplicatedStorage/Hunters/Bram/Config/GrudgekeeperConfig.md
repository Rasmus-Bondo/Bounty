# GrudgekeeperConfig

All tuning numbers for Bram's kit. Values marked PLACEHOLDER in-source are balance assumptions from `bram_implementation_guide.md` and need a playtest pass.

## What it does
1. **Passive**: Health 225 (Thick Hide — highest in roster, no regen/lifesteal outside the ultimate), WalkSpeed 16
2. **Basic** (Grudgekeeper shotgun): 0.5s between shots, 2-round mag, 1s reload, 60-stud max range, `AutoFireMaxDistance` 60 (mobile auto-fire holds fire past this — equals `MaxRange`, so it only blocks shots that literally deal zero; raised from 50 the same day, and the FIRE button is unrestricted), `SpherecastRadius = 2` (thickness of the center cast). Damage: 100/50 head/body inside 20 studs, falling linearly to 25/15 at 60. `ConeHalfAngleDegrees` (7.6°) is the **cone-damage spread** (multiple enemies in the cone all take damage) and the cosmetic hole scatter angle; `PelletCount` (8) is now the **cosmetic bullet-hole count per shot** (no damage) plus the legacy damage-divisor
3. **BreachCharge** (Reinhardt-style pinning charge): 6s cooldown, a 3s steerable forced run (`ChargeStartSpeed` 24 → `ChargePeakSpeed` 42, `ChargeTurnRateDegrees` 125 A/D steering, ground/airborne-grace, `ChargeWallCheckDistance` 5 for the client wall raycast). **`ChargeMaxUpwardSpeed` 52 + `ChargeClimbProbeDistance` 4 + `ChargeClimbRiseThreshold` 0.35** are the climb assist (2026-08-05). The charge moves on a Plane-mode `LinearVelocity` driving X/Z with Y left free, so nothing lifts Bram: pressed into rising ground he fed the whole horizontal push into the contact normal and crawled, and a 40–55° lip stopped the charge short of the wall behind it, releasing the carried victim for `ReleaseDamage` instead of slamming. `ChargeMaxUpwardSpeed` was **0** and read only by a `getBreachVelocity` nothing called — it is the cap now, and the dead function is gone. The ceiling on it is `MovementChecker`'s `MAX_VERTICAL_SPEED` (65): climbing a 50° face at `ChargePeakSpeed` asks for ~50 studs/s, so 52 clears every realistic non-wall face with 13 studs/s of anti-cheat headroom. Anything at or above 55° is caught by the wall test first and slams, so the assist only ever acts on ground the charge is supposed to cross — verified against ramps at known angles, with the handover landing exactly on the `cos(55°)` threshold. Bots need none of it: both `BotController:DashToward` paths move the Humanoid, which climbs slopes natively, which is why the same incline stopped a player Bram and not a bot one. `ChargeCancelLockoutTime` **1** is how long the charge is *committed* for — pressing Q again is the only deliberate cancel (firing commits, jumping is locked for the whole charge), and before this the ability could be thrown away on frame one (launch issue #6). It is read **client-side only**, by `tryBreachCharge` in `GrudgekeeperClient`, and that is not a shortcut: the Q cancel and the automatic airborne exit both report a bare `"End"` to the server, so the server cannot tell them apart, and the airborne one legitimately fires at `ChargeUngroundedGraceTime` (0.55) — well inside the lockout — when Bram charges off a ledge. A server-side gate would therefore keep carrying pinned victims after the client had already stopped driving, until the service's own `Time + 0.75` timeout. There is no exploit surface to protect: ending your own charge early only drops victims for the minor `ReleaseDamage` and still pays the full `Cooldown`, so it is a self-nerf. Keep it below `Time` (a lockout at or past 3 would make the charge uncancellable) and above `ChargeUngroundedGraceTime`, or a ledge-hop would out-run the commitment window. A `Pin` sub-table drives the server logic in BreachChargeService: a **deliberately forgiving** frontal grab box (`BoxDepth` 13 × `BoxHalfWidth` 6 × `BoxHalfHeight` 6, `MinForwardDistance` -1.5 so it also scoops enemies level with / just behind Bram — a plow, not a needle), a `CarryDistance` 6 hold point (far enough ahead that Bram never rams his own captive, so the carried body can't false-trigger the stall), wall detection via forward raycast (`WallCheckDistance` 8 server tick ray / `WallVerifyDistance` 18 to accept a client WallHit — both exclude carried victims so a pinned body can't occlude the wall), and the payoff numbers — `SlamDamage` 50 + `SlamStunDuration` 2.5s on a wall slam, `ReleaseDamage` 15 on a whiff, and `ClashStunDuration` **1.5s** for two charging Brams meeting head-on (launch issue #23 — both charges cancel and both Brams are stunned, with **no knockback, no clash damage and no impact FX**, so this number is the entire cost of the interaction and its only tuning lever). It sits deliberately below `SlamStunDuration`: a slam is a play the victim lost and pays 50 damage on top, whereas a clash is symmetrical, nobody misplayed, and both Brams already forfeit the charge plus a full `Cooldown` restart — and because Redwater is free-for-all, two Brams stunned together in the open are a free double-kill for any third player who did nothing to earn it. Raise it only with that in mind. `SlamWallClearance` 2 is where a slammed victim is seated, measured back from the wall **face**: the intent is "pinned against the wall", and character geometry reaches 1.3-1.7 studs forward of the HumanoidRootPart depending on skin (SummerDust deepest at 1.73), so 2 is flush contact with a small margin. Raising it makes the victim visibly bounce off the wall instead of being slammed into it; it doubles as the back-off margin for the carry/settle clear-space clamp. `CarryLaneSpacing` **1** is the lateral gap between simultaneously-carried victims (lanes: centre, +1, -1, +2 …); before lanes existed every victim on one charge shared a single point. It is deliberately **smaller than a character** (collision is ~2-3 studs wide) so carried victims still overlap — 3.5 was tried first and read as goofy, a comedy-wide row rather than a bruiser hauling bodies. Partial overlap is the intended look, and it is safe at this value **only because the carry is clamped to clear space**: overlapping bodies in open air resolve gently, whereas the original void death needed victims stacked *and* jammed inside geometry with nowhere to eject. Do not raise it for "safety" — the clamp is what makes it safe, not the gap. `WallCastRadius` **1.5** is the radius of the forward wall check's spherecast; the wall test fires a centre ray *and* a sphere of this radius and accepts either, so it catches lattice cover a ray shoots through without losing the rock faces a sphere would reject by clipping a sloped floor first. **It must be identical across all three implementations** — `BreachChargeService._isWallAhead` (server tick + `OnWallHit` re-verify), `BotCombat._isWallAhead` (bots) and `detectBreachWall` in `GrudgekeeperClient` (the client that *reports* the hit) — because the client detecting on a test the server does not share turns every slam it reports into a rejected "no wall" and a 15-damage whiff. Kept `Dash*` keys feed the shared RevolverDashService cooldown/animation path (movement is client-owned)
4. **ShrapnelShell**: 8s cooldown, 100-stud hitscan; inner ring 5.4 studs = 50 dmg + 30% slow, outer 12.2 = 25 dmg + 15% slow, 2s slow
5. **Ultimate** (Last Call): 10s, 50% damage reduction, 25% lifesteal, 0.5s reload / 0.25s between shots, -1s ability cooldown per landed basic shot, +25% move speed
6. **Penetration**: Bram's per-hunter overrides for the shared BulletPenetration rules (`DamageMultiplierPerLayer` 0.5, `MaxLayers` 2), falling back to `BulletPenetration.Config` for anything omitted. The blast resolves as one cone rather than eight independent pellets, so a wallbang applies the multiplier to the whole shot once
7. **LagCompensation** table mirrors Dust/Vesper values; **no HitForgiveness table by design** — the thick spherecast (`SpherecastRadius`) is its own forgiveness
8. **Impact** table: bullet-hole/smoke constants for RevolverImpactFX
