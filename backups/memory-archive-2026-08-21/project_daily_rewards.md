Daily play-streak reward system, built 2026-07-19. **Reworked 2026-07-24** (see rework block below): Day 1 is FREE/instant, Days 2+ still earned by playing ≥1 round on a new reset-day, all claimed in the Bounty lobby.

**Reward track (in shared `ReplicatedStorage.DailyRewardConfig`, mirrored verbatim both places):** D1 50 Cash · D2 200 XP · D3 1 Cash + 1 XP boost item (`cash_25_15`+`xp_25_15`) · D4 100 Cash · D5 400 XP · D6 2 boost items (cash+xp) · D7 50 Tokens + unlock passive · D8+ 10 Tokens each (bumped from 5 on 2026-07-20). Passive = **+10% Cash & XP, active while play-streak ≥ 7**, additive with the 25% [[project_boost_potions]] potions (→ ×1.35), applied in Redwater `BoostService.getMultiplier` reading `data.DailyPermaBoost`.

**Model (user's decisions):** streak driven by **UTC calendar day** (`floor(os.time()/86400)`); "played a game" = **finished a round** (hooked next to `awardRoundCompletion` in Redwater RoundController). Backlog queue: unclaimed rewards stay claimable **the next day** while streak alive. **Breaking the streak (skip a full UTC day) resets to day 1, turns OFF the passive, AND wipes the unclaimed queue** (rewards lost) — enforced lazily via `DailyRewardConfig.applyLapse` on every read/claim in both places.

**Cross-place split (KEY):** earning is Redwater-only, claiming is Bounty-only, over the shared `PlayerData_v2` profile. Profile fields: `DailyStreak`, `DailyLastPlayDay`, `DailyPending` (array of day nums), `DailyPermaBoost`. Redwater `DailyRewardService.registerPlay` advances + queues + fires `DailyRewardEarned` toast. Bounty `DailyRewardService.claim(player, dayNum)` claims **one pending day at a time** (per-day claim, since 2026-07-20 — removes the first queue entry equal to dayNum, ignores a non-pending day): Cash/Tokens via [[project_economy_events]] Wallet (new reason `daily_reward`→TimedReward, added to BOTH Wallet copies), boost items via `BoostService.grantBoost`, and **account XP granted in the lobby** by adding to `data.XP` + recomputing `data.Level` with a **mirrored XP curve** (XP_BASE=250/LINEAR=100/QUADRATIC=0.5, copied from Redwater `RoundSystem.Config`) — the lobby has no ProgressionService.

**2026-07-24 REWORK (free Day 1 + reset countdown):** Two additions on top of the calendar-day engine; the lapse/wipe/passive logic is UNCHANGED. (1) **Free instant Day 1** — new Bounty-side `ensureFreeFirstDay(data)` runs after `applyLapse` in `sendState`/`claim`: when `DailyStreak == 0` (brand-new OR just lapsed) it starts the streak (`DailyStreak=1`, `DailyLastPlayDay=today`) and queues Day 1 so it's instantly claimable WITHOUT playing. Mirrors `registerPlay`'s day-1 bookkeeping → claim path needs no special-casing, Redwater `registerPlay` is a no-op the rest of that day, and because it keys on streak 0 the free Day 1 **returns after every lapse** (user chose "welcome-back", not brand-new-only). Gated by config `FREE_FIRST_DAY=true`. (2) **Live countdown to a GLOBAL reset** — config now has `RESET_OFFSET_HOURS=0` (reset at 00:00 UTC; set e.g. -5 → US-Eastern midnight; server-authoritative, not clock-spoofable — user chose global fixed over per-player local) wired into `dayNumber`, plus `nextResetTime`/`secondsUntilReset` helpers. `DailyRewardState` payload gained `advancedToday` (`DailyLastPlayDay == today`) + `secondsUntilReset`. The client's single NEXT tile now shows a ticking `H:MM:SS` countdown when `advancedToday` (wait for reset), or **"PLAY"** once the reset passed (play a round to earn it); a `RunService.Heartbeat` ticks it off `os.clock()`+`secondsUntilReset` (ignores client wall-clock) and re-requests state at zero.
