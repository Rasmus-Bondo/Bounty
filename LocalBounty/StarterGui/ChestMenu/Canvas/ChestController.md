# ChestController

The LOOT tab's chest scene: purchase panel with odds, the OPEN / SHOOT run, the 3D chest landing, laser shots, bullet reveals, glow, loot reveal and the LOOT-tab bubble. Merged on 2026-09-03 from the "Bounty - UI Update" prototype (the whole UI layer and every feel constant) and the old live copy (the Persistent-model streaming fix).

## What it does
1. **Connects the `LootboxStateEvent` handler before any scene construction** and fires `RequestLootboxState` right after — the profile-load push can beat a long build, and a fire with no listener is dropped for good. The state drives the purchase panel, the held-chest count, the XP progress bar (`ChestProgress.ChestXPBar`) and the bubble
2. **Replays the server's roll instead of rolling.** OPEN fires `OpenLootboxEvent(boxType)`, lands the chest while the reply is in flight, then waits for `LootboxResultEvent` (8s cap). The run lands exactly `stage` bullets, on shot numbers drawn at random once per chest (`shouldShotHit`, the pattern kept on the result) so the last shot can still be the one that decides, and the reveal spawns exactly the items it was given, ascending by tier so the headline lands last. The only `Random` left (`visualRandom`) places miss holes and loot spin phases. A rejected or missing reply resets the chest and re-requests state. Every `resetChestState` bumps `ChestFlow.run`; the shot step and the open sequence capture it and stop after any yield that finds it changed, so a tab change, a fresh chest or a timeout mid-run no longer leaves them driving a track that is gone
3. **Streaming:** `Workspace.PersistentMarkers.ChestShootPart` and `Workspace.SkinMenu.PreviewPart` through `WaitForRequired` — a loose root-level part does not replicate until it streams in, and this wait is at the top of the file
4. **A bought chest banks:** PURCHASE fires `PurchaseLootboxEvent`, the next push (carrying `purchaseResult`) clears the busy flag, and the buy button stays up so a player can stock up. Busy is not a look: the button stays red and labelled PURCHASE, clicks inside `REMOTE_COOLDOWN` (+50 ms) of the last send are dropped client-side so they never meet the server's cooldown, and the no-reply fallback is 3 s. The prototype's OWNED swap is unused; `AfterPurchaseText` shows the held count. A super chest is opened before any standard one, and the title reads SUPER CHEST for it
5. Odds panel painted from `LootboxConfig.DISPLAYED_ODDS` with the authored tier colours, plus two guarantee bars as rows six and seven (`OddsFrame.6PityEpic` / `7PityLegendary`: `Track > Fill` cloned from `ChestProgress.ChestXPBar`, `Text` cloned from the old pity label) that `refreshProgress` fills from the server's `pityEpic` / `pityLegendary` against `LootboxConfig`'s thresholds — a bar empties the moment its guarantee fires; price from `LootboxConfig.PRICE_TOKENS`. A `restricted` state hides the price and greys the button as UNAVAILABLE IN YOUR REGION
6. **Loot visuals** (`buildLootVisual`, since 2026-09-03): a reward floats out as its real model where one exists — a weapon skin from `ReplicatedStorage.WeaponSkins.<hunter>.<skin>` laid in side profile by a per-hunter axis table (`K.LOOT_GUN_AXES`, the same axes `WeaponDisplayConfig` documents), Cash / Tokens as the `MoneyPhysical` coin on its edge — and as a billboard card where none does: the BoostConfig potion icon, the title rendered in its own style through `TitleRenderer.apply`, and a rig — a hero skin from `ReplicatedStorage.Characters.<hunter>.Skin.<skin>` playing the lobby idle (`K.LOOT_RIG_IDLE_ANIMATION`), or an emote on the default Dust — inside a ViewportFrame on the card (`rigCardVisual`, since 2026-09-06: root anchored, every other part free, the Animator posing them in a WorldModel that runs no physics — the in-world emote rig it replaces needed simulated limbs to chase a root the hover moved every frame and never settled into the clip, and the anchored, ScaleTo-shrunk hero rig left Crimson Revenant's layered clothing undeformed and its Model-adorned label studs away at runtime; the clip from `EmoteConfig.get(id).animationId`, prewarmed at build and loaded through `AnimationLoader.load` once the pop lands; the viewport sits in its own ScreenGui (`ChestLootViewports`, DisplayOrder -1) pinned to the carrier every frame through `WorldToViewportPoint`, because a ViewportFrame inside a BillboardGui renders nothing in the live client, and is destroyed with the carrier through `Destroying`; the hover spin orbits the viewport camera so the rig turns like the 3D rewards beside it; `K.LOOT_RIG_CARD_*`, `K.LOOT_EMOTE_CARD_HEIGHT`). An emote name card if the rig or clip is missing. Anything unresolvable falls back to the tier-coloured `LootTemplate` union, the only object ever recoloured. Every reward's tier shows as the chest's own glow behind it from the moment it leaves the chest (`attachLootGlow`, attached at spawn since the fourth round — it used to wait for the landing): a recoloured, rescaled clone of `WorkingChest.GlowPart.Attachment.Rays` playing from an Attachment on an invisible point carrier (a part emitter spawns anywhere in the part's volume, which scattered the rays around the reward; on an attachment they stack at one spot, as the chest's own do), the rays sized to the reward, tuned more visible than the chest's own (rate 2 × lifetime 1.5 s, held at transparency 0.2, `Brightness` 3), parented beside the reward in Workspace (inside a Model reward, the pop's `ScaleTo` blew the rays up 12.5x) and destroyed with it through `Destroying`, kept on the camera's far side by `placeLootGlow` through the pop, the bob and the spin. Particles only: their additive blend hides the ray texture's black ground, which a BillboardGui drew as a black square, and a neon disc read as a coloured circle — both were cut. Models are normalised to a stud extent relative to `GetScale()` (the templates carry baked scales) from the VISIBLE, non-`WrapLayer` parts, and the pivot is re-based on the `PrimaryPart.PivotOffset` because `Model.WorldPivot` is a silent no-op on a model with a PrimaryPart
7. **Loot labels:** each loot object gets a clone of `ReplicatedStorage.Lootbox.LootLabel` (BillboardGui, two lines cloned from the odds labels, each on a dark pill — `NameBack` / `SubBack`, sized from the line's `TextBounds` plus padding, the sub line's only while it has text), raised by the object's half height and, on a Model reward, adorned to a `LabelAnchor` part at its pivot (a Model adornee sits on the bounding box, not the pivot) — the item name in its tier colour, and after a beat the duplicate conversion — `DUPLICATE` in orange, the refund in its currency's colour (Tokens purple, Cash yellow; `K.LOOT_DUPLICATE_HEX` / `K.LOOT_REFUND_HEX`) — so a repeat reads as a near-miss rather than a silent swap
8. **Bubble:** `MainMenuGui.CanvasBottom.Frame.2LootFrame.LootNotifFrame` (a clone of `DailyNotiFrame`, a 0.28 circle on the diamond's apex at `0.5, 0.192` like the STORE tab's `NewItemFrame`) shows `unopened + superUnopened`, capped at `99+`, resolved with `FindFirstChild` so a missing bubble degrades to none. TextScaled, never sized from TextBounds
9. **Menu wiring:** opens on `MenuCameraArrived` / `BlackBarMenuCovered` for `"2LootFrame"` (with a 2s fallback off `SelectedMenuFrame`), closes — destroying the chest, loot and holes — when `SelectedMenuFrame` moves off the tab. Nothing enables the ScreenGui directly
10. **Post-tutorial routing** (spec 7c), profile-driven: when `superPending` is true, waits for `BountyIntroComplete`, then for `TutorialCompleteGui` to show and hide if TeleportData says a card is coming, then fires `MenuNavigateRequested("2LootFrame")` — once per session, so backing out is respected and the next join routes again
11. Every cloned Sound is given the `SFX` SoundGroup; feel constants live in one `K` table because the flat list of ~90 locals plus the new wiring exceeded Luau's 200-register cap
12. **Shot call-out** (`spawnShotText`, owner 2026-09-03): every shot flings a `HIT` (SUCCESS green) or `MISS` (DANGER red) label from just over the chest, never from where the shot landed (`K.SHOT_TEXT_LIFT` of the chest's live height over its base pivot, jittered sideways by `K.SHOT_TEXT_JITTER` so five do not stack), on a throwaway carrier's BillboardGui (the `LootLabel` name label's font and stroke). It starts at a fifth of its size, pops past full, rides a quadratic curve up and out to a random camera side and fades over the last 55% of a 0.9 s flight; Debris removes it. Constants in `K.SHOT_TEXT_*`
13. **Shot camera feel leaves the camera exactly as found:** the lobby camera is parked between tabs, so the shake swaps its previous frame's offset out of `Camera.CFrame` before applying the next and removes it at the end, and the FOV kick holds one rest FOV per run (a cancelled kick's `Completed` no longer starts a stray return tween). Both restore in `resetChestState`. The OPEN / SHOOT button only greys when nothing is held; a chest landing or opening keeps it red
14. **Lobby chrome fade** (`Chrome`, since 2026-09-06): when OPEN is accepted every other ScreenGui under PlayerGui, plus this menu's purchase panel and XP bar, fades out over 0.35 s and is disabled (a panel hidden) once faded, so invisible buttons take no clicks; it fades back in once the loot has landed, and `resetChestState` restores it too. Kept by name (`K.CHROME_KEEP`): `ReworkedSettingsGui`, `VersionTagGui`, `PingFPSGui`, the black-bar transition, `ChestLootViewports` and `ChestMenu` itself. Transparencies are snapshotted per GuiObject / UIStroke and tweened back; a GUI its owner enabled meanwhile is left as set, a GUI with nothing visible is never touched
15. **The purchase panel is scaled up at runtime** (since 2026-09-06). Authored as a fraction of the screen it lands 300 x 486 px at 1080p and 125 x 162 on a landscape Galaxy A06, where the odds rows come out ~9 px tall. A `UIScale` named `PanelScale` grows the panel and all twelve of its children by one number; the frame is first re-anchored from centre-right to **top-right** (its position pulled up by half its height) so the growth runs down and left, away from the settings gear, the version tag and the LOOT currency chips in the band above it. How far is measured off `MainMenuGui.CanvasBottom`, and it is not one constant: the nav bar is aspect-locked, spanning half a phone's width but 0.63 of a 16:9 desktop's, so the larger of two ways past it wins — the panel's bottom stopping 6 px above the nav's top, or its left edge staying 6 px clear of the nav's right edge. A phone gets past it sideways (~1.39), a 16:9 desktop stops above it (~1.25); 1.45 is a guard cap and 1.2 stands in until the nav can be measured. Re-measured on the nav's `AbsoluteSize`, on this GUI's `AbsoluteSize` and on its `Enabled`, because a disabled ScreenGui reports the window it was last enabled in. CurrencyHudController measures this panel's live box, so the LOOT chips follow the growth. Instance reads with fallbacks, not code links
16. **Studio-only loot preview** (since 2026-09-06, gated on `RunService:IsStudio()`): F9 with the LOOT tab open runs `setupChest`, parks the open-lid track on its last frame, hides the chrome and reveals the next scripted batch of five, cycling through every `LootboxConfig.POOLS` entry plus one duplicate per duplicable kind and a made-up kind for the generic fallback, with labels built as `LootboxService.labelFor` builds them. No remote is fired and no chest is consumed. Lazily requires `SkinCatalog` and `TitlesConfig` for the labels and refunds

### RemoteEvents
| Remote | Direction | Other script |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| `RequestLootboxState` | → Server | [[LootboxService]] pull half of the join handshake |
| `LootboxStateEvent` | ← Server | [[LootboxService]] counters, XP progress, restricted flag, price, `purchaseResult` |
| `PurchaseLootboxEvent` | → Server | [[LootboxService]] bank one chest |
| `OpenLootboxEvent` | → Server | [[LootboxService]] roll the chest on the pedestal |
| `LootboxResultEvent` | ← Server | [[LootboxService]] the resolved tier and items to replay |
