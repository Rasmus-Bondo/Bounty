# ChestController

The LOOT tab's chest scene: purchase panel with odds, the OPEN / SHOOT run, the 3D chest landing, laser shots, bullet reveals, glow, loot reveal and the LOOT-tab bubble. Merged on 2026-09-03 from the "Bounty - UI Update" prototype (the whole UI layer and every feel constant) and the old live copy (the Persistent-model streaming fix).

## What it does
1. **Connects the `LootboxStateEvent` handler before any scene construction** and fires `RequestLootboxState` right after — the profile-load push can beat a long build, and a fire with no listener is dropped for good. The state drives the purchase panel, the held-chest count, the XP progress bar (`ChestProgress.ChestXPBar`, hugging the top edge since 2026-09-06: anchored `(0.5, 0)` at `y 0.035`, `0.16 x 0.029`, its chest icon `RelativeYY` so the overhang is height-based) and the bubble
2. **Replays the server's roll instead of rolling.** OPEN fires `OpenLootboxEvent(boxType)`, lands the chest while the reply is in flight, then waits for `LootboxResultEvent` (8s cap). The run lands exactly `stage` bullets, on shot numbers drawn at random once per chest (`shouldShotHit`, the pattern kept on the result) so the last shot can still be the one that decides, and the reveal spawns exactly the items it was given, ascending by tier so the headline lands last. The only `Random` left (`visualRandom`) places miss holes and loot spin phases. A rejected or missing reply resets the chest and re-requests state. Every `resetChestState` bumps `ChestFlow.run`; the shot step and the open sequence capture it and stop after any yield that finds it changed, so a tab change, a fresh chest or a timeout mid-run no longer leaves them driving a track that is gone
3. **Streaming:** `Workspace.PersistentMarkers.ChestShootPart` and `Workspace.SkinMenu.PreviewPart` through `WaitForRequired` — a loose root-level part does not replicate until it streams in, and this wait is at the top of the file
4. **A bought chest banks:** PURCHASE fires `PurchaseLootboxEvent`, the next push (carrying `purchaseResult`) clears the busy flag, and the buy button stays up so a player can stock up. Busy is not a look: the button stays red and labelled PURCHASE, clicks inside `REMOTE_COOLDOWN` (+50 ms) of the last send are dropped client-side so they never meet the server's cooldown, and the no-reply fallback is 3 s. The prototype's OWNED swap is unused; `AfterPurchaseText` shows the held count. A super chest is opened before any standard one, and the title reads SUPER CHEST for it. A `purchaseResult` of `ok` plays the Trading Post's `UIPurchaseSucces` cue from `ReplicatedStorage.SFX.UIButtonClick`, cloned onto SoundService in the `SFX` group (`playPurchaseCue`, since 2026-09-06); `poor` / `restricted` / `cooldown` stay silent
5. Odds panel painted from `LootboxConfig.DISPLAYED_ODDS` with the authored tier colours, plus two guarantee bars as rows six and seven (`OddsFrame.6PityEpic` / `7PityLegendary`: `Track > Fill` cloned from `ChestProgress.ChestXPBar`, `Text` cloned from the old pity label) that `refreshProgress` fills from the server's `pityEpic` / `pityLegendary` against `LootboxConfig`'s thresholds — a bar empties the moment its guarantee fires; price from `LootboxConfig.PRICE_TOKENS`. A `restricted` state hides the price and greys the button as UNAVAILABLE IN YOUR REGION
6. **Loot visuals** (`buildLootVisual`, since 2026-09-03): a reward floats out as its real model where one exists — a weapon skin from `ReplicatedStorage.WeaponSkins.<hunter>.<skin>` laid in side profile by a per-hunter axis table (`K.LOOT_GUN_AXES`, the same axes `WeaponDisplayConfig` documents), Cash / Tokens as the `MoneyPhysical` coin on its edge — and as a billboard card where none does: the BoostConfig potion icon, the title rendered in its own style through `TitleRenderer.apply` on a dark pill sized from its `TextBounds` (`K.LOOT_TITLE_PILL_PAD`; title cards only), and a rig — a hero skin from `ReplicatedStorage.Characters.<hunter>.Skin.<skin>` playing the lobby idle (`K.LOOT_RIG_IDLE_ANIMATION`), or an emote on the default Dust — inside a ViewportFrame on the card (`rigCardVisual`, since 2026-09-06: root anchored, every other part free, the Animator posing them in a WorldModel that runs no physics — the in-world emote rig it replaces needed simulated limbs to chase a root the hover moved every frame and never settled into the clip, and the anchored, ScaleTo-shrunk hero rig left Crimson Revenant's layered clothing undeformed and its Model-adorned label studs away at runtime; the clip from `EmoteConfig.get(id).animationId`, prewarmed at build and loaded through `AnimationLoader.load` once the pop lands; the viewport sits in its own ScreenGui (`ChestLootViewports`, DisplayOrder -1) pinned to the carrier every frame through `WorldToViewportPoint`, because a ViewportFrame inside a BillboardGui renders nothing in the live client, and is destroyed with the carrier through `Destroying`; the hover spin orbits the viewport camera so the rig turns like the 3D rewards beside it; `K.LOOT_RIG_CARD_*`, `K.LOOT_EMOTE_CARD_HEIGHT`). An emote name card if the rig or clip is missing. Anything unresolvable falls back to the tier-coloured `LootTemplate` union, the only object ever recoloured. Every reward's tier shows as the chest's own glow behind it from the moment it leaves the chest (`attachLootGlow`, attached at spawn since the fourth round — it used to wait for the landing): a recoloured, rescaled clone of `WorkingChest.GlowPart.Attachment.Rays` playing from an Attachment on an invisible point carrier (a part emitter spawns anywhere in the part's volume, which scattered the rays around the reward; on an attachment they stack at one spot, as the chest's own do), the rays sized to the reward, tuned more visible than the chest's own (rate 2 × lifetime 1.5 s, held at transparency 0.2, `Brightness` 3), parented beside the reward in Workspace (inside a Model reward, the pop's `ScaleTo` blew the rays up 12.5x) and destroyed with it through `Destroying`, kept on the camera's far side by `placeLootGlow` through the pop, the bob and the spin. Particles only: their additive blend hides the ray texture's black ground, which a BillboardGui drew as a black square, and a neon disc read as a coloured circle — both were cut. Models are normalised to a stud extent relative to `GetScale()` (the templates carry baked scales) from the VISIBLE, non-`WrapLayer` parts, and the pivot is re-based on the `PrimaryPart.PivotOffset` because `Model.WorldPivot` is a silent no-op on a model with a PrimaryPart
7. **Loot labels:** each loot object gets a clone of `ReplicatedStorage.Lootbox.LootLabel` (BillboardGui, two lines cloned from the odds labels), raised by the object's half height and, on a Model reward, adorned to a `LabelAnchor` part at its pivot (a Model adornee sits on the bounding box, not the pivot) — the item name in its tier colour, and after a beat the duplicate conversion — `DUPLICATE` in orange, the refund in its currency's colour (Tokens purple, Cash yellow; `K.LOOT_DUPLICATE_HEX` / `K.LOOT_REFUND_HEX`) — so a repeat reads as a near-miss rather than a silent swap. The billboard's authored size is in screen pixels, so the clone is re-sized against viewport height on spawn and on every resize or camera swap (`K.LOOT_LABEL_REFERENCE_HEIGHT` 720, clamped by `MIN_SCALE` / `MAX_SCALE`)
8. **Bubble:** `MainMenuGui.CanvasBottom.Frame.2LootFrame.LootNotifFrame` (a clone of `DailyNotiFrame`, a 0.28 circle on the diamond's apex at `0.5, 0.192` like the STORE tab's `NewItemFrame`) shows `unopened + superUnopened` PLUS `ReplicatedStorage.ChestArrivalOffset`, clamped at zero and capped at `99+`, resolved with `FindFirstChild` so a missing bubble degrades to none. That offset is ChestArrivalClient's signed withholding of chests it has banked but not yet flown into this tab, so the bubble never counts a chest the player has not been shown arriving; it repaints on the attribute as well as on the state push, and an absent offset reads as 0. Only the bubble is withheld from — `heldCount()` stays the truth for the open flow, so an unflown chest is still openable. TextScaled, never sized from TextBounds
9. **Menu wiring:** opens on `MenuCameraArrived` / `BlackBarMenuCovered` for `"2LootFrame"` (with a 2s fallback off `SelectedMenuFrame`), closes — destroying the chest, loot and holes — when `SelectedMenuFrame` moves off the tab. Nothing enables the ScreenGui directly
10. **Post-tutorial routing** (spec 7c), profile-driven: when `superPending` is true, waits for `BountyIntroComplete`, then for `TutorialCompleteGui` to show and hide if TeleportData says a card is coming, then fires `MenuNavigateRequested("2LootFrame")` — once per session, so backing out is respected and the next join routes again
11. Every cloned Sound is given a SoundGroup, so none of them escapes the settings sliders: `SFX` for the chest's own positional noises, and `Music` for the rare-or-better chime (`LootBoxChime`, since 2026-09-06). The chime starts when the server's roll comes back from OPEN — the earliest the tier can be known, since the client never rolls — and only for `LootboxConfig.TIER_INDEX.Rare` or better, so a good chest sings while it is still landing. It is cloned from `ReplicatedStorage.LootboxSFX` onto SoundService (2D, unlike the chest SFX) and it LOOPS: the clip is barely three seconds, so played once it ran out around the first shot and read as the shot cutting it off. It carries the whole run and `playChestOpenSFX` fades it over `K.CHIME_FADE_TIME` as the lid opens, handing the moment to the open SFX. Looping is why it has no Debris timer — a scavenge would cut a run short mid-shot; the lifetime bound is `resetChestState`, which hard-cuts it on every exit path, so it never rings on over the lobby after the tab closes. Feel constants live in one `K` table because the flat list of ~90 locals plus the new wiring exceeded Luau's 200-register cap
12. **Shot call-out** (`spawnShotText`, owner 2026-09-03): every shot flings a `HIT` (SUCCESS green) or `MISS` (DANGER red) label from just over the chest, never from where the shot landed (`K.SHOT_TEXT_LIFT` of the chest's live height over its base pivot, jittered sideways by `K.SHOT_TEXT_JITTER` so five do not stack), on a throwaway carrier's BillboardGui (the `LootLabel` name label's font and stroke). It starts at a fifth of its size, pops past full, rides a quadratic curve up and out to a random camera side and fades over the last 55% of a 0.9 s flight; Debris removes it. Constants in `K.SHOT_TEXT_*`
13. **Shot camera feel leaves the camera exactly as found:** the lobby camera is parked between tabs, so the shake swaps its previous frame's offset out of `Camera.CFrame` before applying the next and removes it at the end, and the FOV kick holds one rest FOV per run (a cancelled kick's `Completed` no longer starts a stray return tween). Both restore in `resetChestState`. The OPEN / SHOOT button only greys when nothing is held and reads SHOOT TO OPEN once the chest has landed; a chest landing or opening keeps it red
14. **Lobby chrome fade** (`Chrome`, since 2026-09-06; reworked 2026-09-07): when OPEN is accepted every other ScreenGui under PlayerGui, plus this menu's purchase panel, XP bar and BOUNTY+ panel, fades out over 0.35 s and fades back in once the loot has landed; `resetChestState` restores it too. Kept by name (`K.CHROME_KEEP`): `ReworkedSettingsGui`, `VersionTagGui`, `PingFPSGui`, the black-bar transition, `ChestLootViewports` and `ChestMenu` itself. Transparencies are snapshotted per GuiObject / UIStroke / **UIShadow** and tweened back — UIShadow was missed until 2026-09-07, so ~20 panel shadows held full strength through the whole fade and vanished only when their root was disabled. **Nothing is enabled or disabled any more:** a fade only moves transparencies, and input is stopped separately by `Chrome.lock`, which clears `Interactable` on the buttons and `Active` on the objects that sink clicks into 3D (an Active GuiObject would otherwise swallow shots at the chest). The old path disabled each faded ScreenGui once the fade ended and enabled it again to fade back in, and that enabling was the most expensive thing in the run — a full relayout per GUI plus a 932-instance ViewportFrame rebuild for the BOUNTY+ panel, all landing on the frame the fade-in started. Two further costs went with it: the roots were found by walking all ~17,900 PlayerGui instances to locate eight (8–29 ms, and it ran twice per chest — the walk now prunes at the ScreenGui / GuiObject boundary, still descending non-GUI containers because `ReworkedMainMenuFolder` holds `MainMenuGui`), and invisible subtrees were snapshotted and tweened, the rank leaderboard and the gamemode picker being modals whose entire canvas sits `Visible = false`. 1463 instances walked and 339 tweens became 276 and 186. **Side effect worth knowing:** the fade no longer toggles `MainMenuGui.Enabled`, so listeners in ShopController, CurrencyHudController and FriendsInMatchClient that fired on every chest run no longer do
15. **The purchase panel is scaled up at runtime** (since 2026-09-06). Authored as a fraction of the screen it lands 300 x 486 px at 1080p and 125 x 162 on a landscape Galaxy A06, where the odds rows come out ~9 px tall. A `UIScale` named `PanelScale` grows the panel and all twelve of its children by one number; the frame is first re-anchored from centre-right to **top-right** (its position pulled up by half its height) so the growth runs down and left, away from the settings gear, the version tag and the LOOT currency chips in the band above it. How far is measured off `MainMenuGui.CanvasBottom`, and it is not one constant: the nav bar is aspect-locked, spanning half a phone's width but 0.63 of a 16:9 desktop's, so the larger of two ways past it wins — the panel's bottom stopping 6 px above the nav's top, or its left edge staying 6 px clear of the nav's right edge. A phone gets past it sideways (~1.39), a 16:9 desktop stops above it (~1.25); 1.45 is a guard cap and 1.2 stands in until the nav can be measured. Re-measured on the nav's `AbsoluteSize`, on this GUI's `AbsoluteSize` and on its `Enabled`, because a disabled ScreenGui reports the window it was last enabled in. CurrencyHudController measures this panel's live box, so the LOOT chips follow the growth. **The panel is also pushed DOWN far enough to leave a band for those chips** (`reservePanelTop` / `PANEL_CHIP_BAND`, 2026-09-07): the push is computed from the panel's AUTHORED width times the scale cap rather than its live width, or the measurement would feed back into the `UIScale` that produced it. On a phone that is ~28 px, which clears the settings gear above and the version tag beside; on a desktop and a 4:3 tablet it is zero. Instance reads with fallbacks, not code links
16. **Studio-only loot preview** (since 2026-09-06, gated on `RunService:IsStudio()`): L with the LOOT tab open runs `setupChest`, parks the open-lid track on its last frame, hides the chrome and reveals the next scripted batch of five, cycling through every `LootboxConfig.POOLS` entry plus one duplicate per duplicable kind and a made-up kind for the generic fallback, with labels built as `LootboxService.labelFor` builds them. No remote is fired and no chest is consumed. Lazily requires `SkinCatalog` and `TitlesConfig` for the labels and refunds
17. **Owns the BOUNTY+ panel** (2026-09-06) — `GamepassPurchaseFrame`, the mirror of the chest panel on the LEFT of the LOOT area, folded in here rather than given a second controller because two owners of purchase state desync the moment a request is refused. Added as one `do` block so it contributes no file-level locals, and every lookup is guarded so a missing frame leaves the LOOT tab untouched. It: grows the panel with a `UIScale` about the TOP-LEFT corner, bound by the nav diamonds' LEFT edge (the mirror of the chest panel's rule, re-derived rather than copied — the nav is centred, so this side's clearance is its own measurement); fetches the live price with `InfoType.GamePass` and draws it through `ShopCore.attachRobuxPrice` as the Robux IMAGE, showing nothing rather than a zero while the pass is unpriced; prompts `PromptGamePassPurchase` from a hitbox over the authored Frame; and swaps BuyButton → OwnedButton off the `OwnsBountyPlus` attribute and its change signal, so the panel flips the instant the purchase lands. Since 2026-09-07 it also **fades with the rest of the lobby chrome on OPEN**, as a fade root in its own right rather than riding on `ChestMenu`, which stays in `K.CHROME_KEEP` because the SHOOT button lives there. Its `ViewportFrame` contents are not driven from here — DisplayCharacterAnimator, a separate LocalScript inside the frame, owns the rig and the camera
