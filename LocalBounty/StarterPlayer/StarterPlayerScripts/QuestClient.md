# QuestClient

Drives the designed `StarterGui.QuestGui` panel. The **DAILY** tab shows daily quests + a complete-all bonus from [[QuestService]] (claimable here). The **WEEKLY** tab shows the **battle-pass quests** ([[BattlePassConfig]] `WEEKS`) **one week at a time**, picked from a second tab row (`VIEW PASS | WEEK 1 | WEEK 2 | WEEK 3`), each **claimable**: Redwater only marks them completed, and the XP is collected here via [[BattlePassService]]. The old QuestConfig weekly system is disabled (`WEEKLY_COUNT = 0`).

## What it does
1. Builds `questById` from [[QuestConfig]] (daily) and holds `QUEST_TITLE`, **one** category map covering both engines. It used to be two maps (`TYPE_TITLE` for daily, `BP_TITLE` for the pass) — two places to forget, which is how one event ended up named `gamesPlayed` on the pass side and `roundComplete` on the daily side. A type with no entry falls back to `"QUEST"`/`"WEEKLY"` rather than erroring, so a config entry can land before its label does
1b. `QUEST_TITLE.hunterVariety = "SWAP ON DEATH"` deliberately carries the *how*, not a category. The objective slot is a `TextScaled` box ~86×13 px; "Use 3 different hunters in one match — swap when you die" needs 169px there and TextScaled resolves that by wrapping to two ~6px lines. The title slot is twice the height, so the explanation lives there and the objective stays short. Swapping is death-gated and only offered on the kill-cam card row, so a player who has not died mid-match has no way to know the feature exists
1b2. **The three bounty types carry three distinct titles.** `bountyClaim` read "BOUNTIES" while `becomeBounty` and `bountyTime` both read "MOST WANTED", so a card heading gave no hint which side of the bounty its quest was about — hunting the marked player or being them. They are now `BOUNTY HUNTER` / `MOST WANTED` / `TIME AS BOUNTY`, and `ultimateKill` split off `ultimateUse` as `ULTIMATE KILLS`. The titles do half the disambiguating work; the labels in [[BattlePassConfig]] and [[QuestConfig]] do the other half
1c. `formatCount(n)` abbreviates the progress readout above 1,000 (`12k / 15k`, `8.4k / 15k`). That readout sits in a **29×23 px** box, so a raw `12000 / 15000` needs 2.5× the width it has and TextScaled resolves it by wrapping into an unreadable blob — visible the moment the damage and bounty-time quests landed, since those carry goals in the thousands. Rounds **down** above 10k so the readout can never claim more progress than was earned
1c. **Scoped quest types are keyed exactly.** `QUEST_TITLE` carries `["kill:tdm"]`, `["roundComplete:domination"]`, `["killAs:Dust"]` and the rest as literal keys, not a prefix match — an unlisted type falls back to `"WEEKLY"`, so a missing entry costs a dull card rather than a broken one. Naming them is what stops three `Get 50 kills as …` cards all sitting under one heading reading ELIMINATIONS. Fortuna has no `killAs` entry because she has no `killAs` quest (see [[BattlePassConfig]])
2. Resolves the authored `PlayerGui.QuestGui` hierarchy (`Canvas.QuestFrame` → `OptionFrame` tabs, `OptionFrameWeekly` week tabs, `QuestListFrame` daily grid, `WeeklyQuestListFrame` scroll, `DailyResetText`), sets it `Enabled=false`, `DisplayOrder=10`, and adds a transparent full-screen `CloseOverlay` for click-outside-to-close. The ScreenGui is authored `ScreenInsets = None` to match `MainMenuGui`: the panel's `Canvas` is positioned so its left edge lands on scale x = 0, and under the previous `DeviceSafeInsets` that zero was the safe-area edge — so on a phone the panel stopped short of the screen while the left menu it slides out of did not
2b. **`fitTopbar` keeps the tab row clear of the Roblox chrome.** The flip side of `ScreenInsets.None` is that nothing holds the panel out from under the Roblox menu / chat / voice buttons, and on a phone the `DAILY | WEEKLY | EVENT` row sat at screen y ~49px while those buttons run to ~55px. The shift is measured rather than branched on device: `GuiService:GetGuiInset()` gives the real topbar height, and the canvas top moves down only by the **deficit** — how far the tab row falls short of clearing it, divided by `1 - tabsTopFraction` because shrinking the canvas pulls the tabs back up. Desktop's tab row already sits ~134px down, so the deficit is zero and nothing moves there. The canvas keeps its bottom edge, so the panel compresses instead of sliding off-screen; authored position/size are captured up front, since re-reading them would measure the shift already applied. The `QUESTS` header label is separately authored **right**-aligned at the far end of `TopPanel` (it was left-aligned, directly under the Roblox buttons)
3. **Daily** grid = `1Quest`/`2Quest`/`3Quest` + `4CompleteQuest` bonus, rendered from `currentQuestData.Daily`; each card shows category title, objective, progress bar, XP + Cash, and a claim button (CLAIM / CLAIMED / hidden). Claims fire `ClaimQuestRewardEvent` (slot index or `"bonus"`)
4. **Weekly** = **one battle-pass week on screen at a time**, chosen by the `OptionFrameWeekly` row, one card per quest. The panel is authored as 3 pages of 4 identical cards, so on boot each page is grown to `QUESTS_PER_WEEK` (7) by cloning its card template, cards get explicit `LayoutOrder`, and pages are cloned too if a future season runs more weeks than the panel was drawn with. Each card shows BP category title, objective, progress bar + `progress / goal`, the `+XP` reward (cash chip hidden), and a **CLAIM / CLAIMED** button; clicking fires `ClaimBattlePassQuestEvent(questId)` with the card's stamped `refs.questId`. Every page is filled on each refresh, not just the visible one, so a week switch is a `Visible` flip with the cards already drawn. Cards carry **no week prefix**: the selected week tab directly above the list names the week, and the title box is tight enough on a phone that `WEEK 2 · ELIMINATIONS` spent most of the category's room saying it twice
4b. **The card's icon diamond is fitted per card rather than left at its authored size.** `IconFrame` is `SizeConstraint = RelativeYY`, so the diamond is sized off the card's **height**, while `TextHolderFrame`'s left edge is a fraction of the card's **width** — the two close on each other as the viewport narrows, and they were authored to meet at almost exactly 16:9 (break-even aspect **1.7735**; measured clearance 0.14px at 1920×1080, 0.09px at 1280×720, 0.06px on a 16:9 phone). Every narrower aspect overlapped: −10px on a 4:3 iPad, −13px on an iPad Pro, with the diamond drawn through the quest title and objective. `fitCardIcon` measures each card and takes `min(authored 0.6, what fits)`, so PC and phone stay untouched at 0.600 while a 4:3 tablet lands on 0.451, an iPad Air on 0.487 and a PC windowed to 1.6 on 0.541. It is a **measurement, not a device branch**, which is why a narrow PC window is covered too. Two traps it exists to encode: the diamond is a square **rotated 45°**, so `AbsoluteSize` reports the unrotated box and its visual half-width is the half-**diagonal** (41% larger than the property suggests); and under `RelativeYY` one `Size` scale drives both axes off the parent's height. No extra gap is subtracted — PC has only 0.14px of slack, so any gap worth having would shrink the icon on the two devices that already look right. Re-run on canvas resize, tab switch, week switch and panel open; a card inside a hidden week page has no `AbsoluteSize` to measure and keeps its authored size until it is on screen
4c. **The XP and Cash reward icons are fitted too, and by shrinking rather than moving.** Each hangs off its own number's `TextLabel` and carries the same axis mismatch as 4b one level down: the icon is `RelativeYY`, so it is sized off the label's **height** — the axis `TextScaled` also sizes the digits from — while its `-0.0926` x-offset is a fraction of the label's **width**. `QuestGui.Canvas` has no aspect lock, so a 4:3 tablet gives the label 25 % less width for the same height and the icon closes on the digits by exactly that. Measured clearance as a fraction of the icon's own width: XP −0.066 at 16:9 → −0.174 at 4:3, Cash −0.138 → −0.229 (negative at every aspect because both PNGs carry transparent margin, so the **change** is the defect, not the sign). `fitRewardIcons` multiplies the authored `Size` by `min(1, aspect / (16/9))`, which restores the authored gap-to-icon ratio exactly — it falls out as a plain multiply because the offset is the only width-driven term. **Shrunk, not nudged left, because left is where it cannot go**: at 1920×1080 the XP icon already starts 1.2px past the objective label's text and the cash icon 0.7px past the progress readout, so taking room from that side only trades one collision for another; shrinking instead gives room back. Unlike 4b this needs **no measurement**, so it is deliberately **not** part of the `fitAllCardIcons` sweep and does not care whether a week page has ever been shown — it binds to `ViewportSize` and runs over all 16 authored cards (4 daily + 3 weeks × 4; none are cloned). The `min()` is what keeps it a no-op: at 16:9 and wider the authored size is written back unchanged, so every phone and every non-windowed desktop is untouched, while a desktop windowed narrower than 16:9 is corrected — the same call 4b makes
4d. **The cash reward icon is the game's actual cash coin.** All 16 `3CashRewardFrame` icons drew `rbxassetid://114385868451364`, a stack of blue notes that appears nowhere else in the game, while every currency pill — the Trading Post's, the vault's and the Bounty Pass's — draws the gold `$` coin `rbxassetid://78272095076720`. They now draw the coin. Authored property change on all platforms, not a script one, so it travels in the place file and not in a script diff. The authored `1.2` box (against XP's `1.0`) is unchanged: it was checked against the new art by rendering rather than re-derived
5. Weekly progress comes from the battle-pass state: unlocked weeks = `bpState.week`, per-quest progress is `bpState.quests[questId]` (`progress`/`completed`/`claimed`), delivered via `BattlePassDataEvent`. A quest from any already-unlocked week stays claimable for the rest of the season
5b. **`seasonPending()` — the pre-season state** (2026-08-27). Before `SEASON_START` the server reports week 0 and refuses to progress or claim anything, so `refreshAll` draws **no** weekly cards at all. It is deliberately not folded into `unlockedWeeks()`, which keeps its floor of 1: the week **tab row** keeps its shape and only the cards go away, leaving the countdown (item 10) the whole tab. Before this, that floor of 1 rendered seven week-1 cards that silently never moved for the entire gap between a publish and its season start — the standing cost of closing `TEST_ALL_WEEKS_UNTIL`, which now has to be `nil` on any build shipping ahead of its start date
6. `showWeekPage(week)` puts exactly one week on the canvas and sizes the scroll so a weekly card comes out exactly as tall as a daily card, with the canvas ending flush on the last card. Scale inside a ScrollingFrame resolves against the **canvas**, while `CanvasSize`'s own scale resolves against the frame's **parent**, so as fractions of `QuestFrame`: `card = dailyFrameScaleY / 4`, `page = 7 × card`, `CanvasSize.Y = page / weeklyFrameScaleY`, outer grid cell `= 1`. One week is on the canvas, so the canvas **is** the page (measured: weekly card 0.1566 = daily card 0.1566, 0.00 dead scroll). Hidden pages drop out of the grid entirely. Switching weeks returns the scroll to the top, but re-selecting the week already shown is a no-op, so an ordinary refresh never moves the player's scroll
7. Selecting **WEEKLY** lands on the week the pass is currently on (`bpState.week`, else `BattlePassConfig.currentWeek()`) rather than on whichever week was read last — that is where the live quests are. There is **no auto-scroll**: week-to-week travel used to be a hand-stepped `Heartbeat` glide from week 1 down to the current week, which needed a cancel token and an input hook to hand the scroll back the moment the player touched it. A week is a tab click now, so the scroll belongs to the player outright and the only write to `CanvasPosition` is the reset-to-top inside `showWeekPage`
8. `refreshAll` re-renders daily (from `currentQuestData`) and weekly (from `bpState`) together; called whenever either payload arrives
9. **Two tab rows.** The main row (`1Daily`/`2Weekly`/`3Event`) swaps the visible list and the selected-tab gradient; **Event** is switchable but intentionally empty. The `OptionFrameWeekly` row (`0ViewPass`/`1Week`/`2Week`/`3Week`) shows on the WEEKLY tab only — on DAILY it would sit on top of the daily grid, which starts higher up the frame (y 0.155) than the weekly list (0.200) the row was authored into the gap above. The row is built from `BattlePassConfig.TOTAL_WEEKS`: it clones a tab if a season runs longer than the three drawn and parks the surplus if shorter, holding the authored 0.95 row span across a different cell count (4 cells at 0.21875 + 3 × 0.025 gaps). A week that has not unlocked yet is **dimmed and inert** — faded caption (`TextTransparency` 0.6) and outline (0.55), background left opaque because fading it lets the panel show through and the tab stops reading as a tab at all. The lock is checked **inside the click handler**, not through `GuiObject.Active`: a GuiButton still fires `MouseButton1Click` with `Active = false`, so `Active` would look like a lock without being one
10. Reset/week countdown: DAILY shows the daily reset countdown; WEEKLY shows the current battle-pass week (`BOUNTY PASS WEEK N OF M` on the final week, else `BOUNTY PASS WEEK N — NEXT UNLOCKS IN …`), or, before the season starts, `BOUNTY PASS QUESTS RESET IN: hh:mm` against `SEASON_START` (5b). The existing 1s countdown loop ticks all three
11. **Three scopes of one claimable signal**, all computed in `updateQuestNotif` from the same counts so they can never disagree. The **CanvasLeft `2Quests` badge** counts daily claimables **plus** completed-and-unclaimed battle-pass quests across every unlocked week — "something is waiting somewhere in here". A **red dot** (authored `TabNotifDot` frame) on the **DAILY** and **WEEKLY** tab buttons answers for its own tab only. A dot on each **WEEK** tab answers for that week alone, so the WEEKLY tab's "somewhere in the pass" resolves to *which* week without the player opening all three; `weeklyClaimableCount(week)` is the same function with an optional week argument, which is what keeps the scopes consistent. `0ViewPass` has no dot (it is a link, not a tab that can hold a reward) and EVENT has none authored, and the resolve is a `FindFirstChild` so a missing key is simply skipped. Nothing clears these by hand: both claim paths echo fresh state from the server ([[QuestService]] re-fires `QuestDataEvent` after a quest or bonus claim, [[BattlePassService]] calls `sendState` after a weekly claim), which lands in `refreshAll` → `updateQuestNotif`. The badge is guarded rather than early-returned on — it lives in a different ScreenGui and resolves late, and the old `if not questNotifFrame then return end` would have left the tab dots stale
11b. **The dot is authored dead-centre on the button's top-right corner** (`AnchorPoint (0.5, 0.5)` at `Position (1, 0)`), so it half-overhangs diagonally. Size is `{1, 0}, {0.5, 0}` with a 1:1 `UIAspectRatioConstraint`: `FitWithinMaxSize` fits the square inside the *whole* Size box, so the X term is a **ceiling** rather than a width and the height term binds — the dot comes out round at half the button height on every device (17.4px at 1658×678, 9.6px on an iPhone 7). An X scale of `0` does **not** work: `FitWithinMaxSize` clamps on both axes and yields 0×0
11c. **`layoutTabDots()` pulls a dot left only by the amount that would have overlapped the next tab.** The overhang into the inter-tab gap is the problem: that gap scales with viewport **width** while the dot scales with button **height**, so the overhang outgrows the gap as the aspect narrows — it clears at 2.45:1 but would run ~1.5px into the WEEKLY button on a phone and **3.8px** on a 4:3 tablet. Shrinking the dot to survive the worst case would make it small on every screen, and pinning its right edge to the button's edge (tried first) visibly tucked it inside the corner. So the position is corrected instead of the size: measure the real `room` to the next tab, subtract the dot's radius plus its `UIStroke.Thickness`, and offset by the shortfall — **zero on any screen with room for it** (measured: 0.0px pull at 2.45:1, 2.0px on an iPhone 7, 4.0px on a 4:3 iPad, all leaving ≥0.2px daylight). Runs on `Canvas.AbsoluteSize` changes and is `task.defer`-ed on first open, because `AbsoluteSize` is 0 until the panel has rendered once — the panel boots `Enabled = false`. EVENT is the last tab and has no dot, so the loop's `nextBg` guard skips it
12. Opened/closed by the main menu's `CanvasLeft.Frame.2Quests.Button`; on open it eager-fetches both quest and battle-pass state. `openPanel(tab)` takes the tab to land on, defaulting to daily — the CanvasLeft button is a general "open quests", while the pass's banner asks for `"weekly"` so a player who taps it arrives on the quests it just advertised
12b. **Two links tie this panel to the Bounty Pass**, which had none in either direction. **In:** `QuestPanelRequested`, a BindableEvent this script publishes on `MainMenuGui` and [[BattlePassClient]] fires — modelled on `MenuNavigateRequested`, where the script that *owns* a surface exposes the event so callers never learn how it opens. **Out:** `0ViewPass` ("VIEW PASS ›"), the **first cell of the WEEK row**, so it rides that row's weekly-only visibility and needs none of its own. It routes through `MenuNavigateRequested("4BattlePassFrame")` rather than enabling `BattlepassGui` directly, because the pass opens on `MenuCameraArrived` — anything that skips the camera flight leaves it shut. The cell is a **clone of a week tab**, so its shell, corner, stroke and caption metrics match the row exactly, and it keeps the authored orange instead of joining the selected/deselected swap: it is an action, not a week you can be looking at
12b-i. **The reset label keeps its authored full width on every tab.** It used to borrow a narrow box on the weekly tab to get out from under `PassLinkButton`, which was a standalone button parked at the right end of the reset row (x 0.685 → 0.985) overlapping the label's full-width box — `TextScaled` sizes text off box **height** until the text runs out of width, so the weekly string drew ~440px on a 1080p PC and ran a good 90px underneath it, and the workaround cost the countdown ~30% of its size there. With the button moved into the WEEK row nothing overlaps the label, so the box and the workaround are both gone
12c. **A weekly card's reward block is headed `BOUNTY PASS XP`, a daily card's `REWARDS`.** Both cards drew the same star icon over the same bare number, so nothing said that a weekly quest pays *battle-pass* XP while a daily one pays *account* XP — two different currencies rendered identically. The heading is the only slot with the room to say it (measured 0.43 of its box; "REWARDS" was 0.27), which is why the fix lives there rather than in the number
13. **Click-outside-to-close is decided by a rect test, not by "the overlay was clicked".** Frames and TextLabels don't consume input unless `Active`, so a press that missed a CLAIM button fell through the card to the full-screen `CloseOverlay` behind it and shut the panel — constantly, on a touchscreen. `overlay.InputBegan` (mouse + touch) now closes only when the press falls outside `Canvas`'s absolute rect; the ScreenGui is `ScreenInsets.None`, so `AbsolutePosition` and `input.Position` share one screen space and no `GetGuiInset` correction applies. `SidePanel` is deliberately subtracted from that rect: it is a scrim over the main menu's left nav column, and at `DisplayOrder` 10 against MainMenuGui's 1 the overlay is the only thing that can hear a press there — treating the strip as *outside* is what keeps a second press on QUESTS able to close the panel, since the panel has no X button. The settings panel (SettingsController) guards the same way and for the same reason

### RemoteEvents
| Remote | Direction | Other script |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| `QuestDataEvent` | ← Server | [[QuestService]] fires full daily quest state on join and after claim |
| `ClaimQuestRewardEvent` | → Server | [[QuestService]] handles daily reward grant (quest slot or `"bonus"`) |
| `RequestQuestDataEvent` | → Server | [[QuestService]] re-sends quest state on load and on panel open |
