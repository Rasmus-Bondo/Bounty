## Update 1 — Frostbite Claim

### 🗺️ New map: Frostbite Claim
- **A frozen claim joins Redwater Canyon** — same gunfight, new ground. Matchmaking picks your map when you press Play.
- **The cold closes in from every side** — Frostbite's danger zone is a shrinking ring, not two walls. Watch your flanks.

### 🗳️ Map voting
- **Vote for the next map at the end of every match** — tap a tile on the vote screen after the podium.
- **A winning vote moves the whole server** — your group travels together and lands on the new map as one. A tie stays put.
- **Friends can still find you** — joining a friend from the app or friends list lands you in their match, wherever it has moved to.

### ❄️ Winter skins
- **Three new hunter skins in the Trading Post** — Bluefrost Dust (300 Tokens), Frostveil Vesper (600) and Direwolf Bram (600), Bram's first skin ever.
- **Frostpiercer (400)** — Vesper's first weapon skin, on the rifle in your hands and on your back.
- **"Let the Hunt Begin" bundle** — Frostveil Vesper and the Frostpiercer together, for Robux.

### ⚙️ Improved
- **Death cam** — dying to the fire or a fall now gives you a camera you can look around with, instead of one frozen shot.

### 🐛 Fixed
- Some revolver sounds ignored the volume sliders entirely — every sound now respects your audio settings.
- The EQUIP / BUY NOW label in the Trading Post rendered tiny on desktop.

See you in the snow! ❄️
