# Battle Pass Design

## Structure

- **Season length:** 4 weeks
- **Total levels:** 40
- **XP per level:** 2,500
- **Total XP to complete:** 100,000

Two tiers: **Free Pass** (small non-premium rewards + token drip) and **Full Pass** (bought with tokens, full reward track).

---

## Weekly Progression Loop

Each week unlocks new quests and is scoped to 10 levels which requires 25,000 XP.

| Phase | Games | XP |
|-------|-------|----|
| Complete weekly quests | ~10 | ~23,100 |
| Extra gameplay to fill gap | ~12 | ~1,920 |
| **Weekly total** | **~22 games** | **~25,000** |

~22 games at 6 minutes each ≈ **2.2 hours/week**.

---

## XP Sources

### Gameplay XP (per game, ~160 XP average)

| Action | XP |
|--------|----|
| Game completion | 25 |
| Kill | 4 |
| Assist | 2 |
| Become a bounty | 20 |
| Kill a bounty | 10 |

Average player (~20 kills, 1–2 bounty claims, 1–2 times becoming bounty) earns **125–200 XP per game**.

### Weekly Quest XP

Total quest XP per week: **21,500**

| Tier | Count | XP each | Subtotal |
|------|-------|---------|---------|
| Easy | 4 | 2,000 | 8,000 |
| Medium | 2 | 3,500 | 7,000 |
| Hard | 1 | 6,500 | 6,500 |
| **Total** | | | **21,500** |

## Weekly Quests

| Quest | Objective | XP |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| Saddle Up | Play 3 games | 2,000 |
| Quick Draw | Get 40 kills | 2,000 |
| Cover Fire | Get 10 assists | 2,000 |
| Claim Your Prize | Claim 2 bounties | 2,000 |
| Gunslinger | Get 100 kills | 3,500 |
| Most Wanted | Become a bounty 6 times | 3,500 |
| **Dead Man's Hand** | **While marked as a bounty, kill 2 players before being taken down — do this 3 times** | **6,500** |

Dead Man's Hand stands out because it flips the script — instead of hunting bounties, the player *is* the bounty under pressure. Requires becoming a bounty and outgunning the players hunting you. Likely takes the full 10 games to complete.
